Window mosquito nets cover open windows with fine mesh. Air flows freely. Insects stop at the barrier.

  • Bedrooms at night
  • Kitchens while cooking
  • Homes without built-in screens

What Actually Matters

  • Mesh fine enough for mosquitoes
