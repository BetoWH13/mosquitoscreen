Storm-Prep Checklist

A two-minute checklist that prevents 90% of outdoor net damage.

Before Heavy Wind or Rain

  • Roll or tie nets open.
  • Remove detachable panels.
  • Secure loose cords.
  • Clear furniture edges.

After the Storm

  • Allow nets to dry fully.
  • Check hooks and ties.
  • Inspect for snags.
