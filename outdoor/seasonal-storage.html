Seasonal Removal & Storage

Simple care at season’s end keeps your mosquito nets lasting for years.

When to Remove Nets

  • End of mosquito season.
  • Before winter storms.
  • When outdoor use slows.

Cleaning Before Storage

  • Rinse with gentle water spray.
  • Use mild soap if needed.
  • Allow to dry completely.

Storing Properly

  • Fold loosely.
  • Store in breathable bag.
  • Keep away from moisture.

Next Season Setup

  • Inspect for damage.
  • Rehang evenly.
  • Replace worn ties.
