Why Nets Tear

  • Uneven hanging tension.
  • Flapping in strong wind.
  • Snagging on furniture corners.
  • Storing nets while damp.

Simple Prevention Tips

  • Use evenly spaced top hooks.
  • Add bottom weights.
  • Tie back nets when not needed.
  • Remove nets before storms.

Extend Lifespan

  • Store dry and folded.
  • Avoid sharp edges nearby.
  • Clean gently with mild soap.
