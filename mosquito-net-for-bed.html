Why Use a Bed Mosquito Net?

Bed nets create a light physical barrier that keeps mosquitoes away while allowing airflow. They are widely used in warm climates, travel accommodations, and homes without window screening.

The key is choosing the right size, material, and hanging method so the net works reliably every night.
