Bed Net Sizing Guide

Correct sizing is the difference between comfort and frustration.

Measure Your Bed

  • Width of mattress
  • Length of mattress
  • Height from mattress to ceiling

Allow Hanging Clearance

Nets need space to drape without touching skin during sleep.

Common Mistakes

  • Choosing nets too short
  • Forgetting ceiling height
  • Not allowing tuck-in space
