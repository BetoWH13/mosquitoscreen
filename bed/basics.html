Bed Net Basics

A clear starting point before choosing a mosquito net for your bed.

What Bed Nets Actually Do

Bed mosquito nets create a breathable mesh barrier that prevents mosquitoes from reaching you while sleeping. They do not trap heat and allow normal airflow.

Main Bed Net Styles

  • Canopy style: Drapes fully around the bed.
  • Box frame style: Stands away from skin contact.
  • Pop-up travel nets: Foldable and portable.

What Bed Nets Do Well

  • Block mosquito bites.
  • Allow airflow.
  • Work without chemicals.

What They Don’t Do

  • They don’t cool the room.
  • They don’t replace window screens.
  • They must be tucked or sealed properly.
