Bed Net Materials

Material affects durability, airflow, and lifespan.

Nylon

  • Lightweight
  • Durable
  • Dries quickly

Polyester

  • Softer feel
  • Wrinkle-resistant
  • Common in home use

Cotton

  • Natural material
  • Heavier drape
  • Less common today
